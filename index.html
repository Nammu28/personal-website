My Story

A little about who I am and what shapes the way I move through life and work.

My journey started in Kuwait, but as a teenager, I packed up my life and moved to the U.S.—a leap of faith that completely reshaped how I see the world. Since then, I’ve been on a continuous path of learning, working and constantly adapting to new environments.

Through every move and milestone, the thing I value most is connection. I genuinely love building meaningful relationships with people from all walks of life. I believe every conversation is a chance to learn something new, and I try to bring intentionality and purpose to everything I do and participate in.

For me, life is all about showing up for others and staying active — mind and body:

Whether I'm collaborating with clients on a business strategy, serving my neighbourhood, or teaching dance classes, I’m driven by a desire to leave things a little better than I found them.

My Values

I’m sharing my core values here because I believe the best work happens when personal purpose aligns with an organization's mission.

Focus

Maintaining clarity on key priorities allows me to deliver high-quality work, minimize distractions, and respect everyone's time through efficient, intentional collaboration.

Health

Prioritizing physical and mental well-being ensures I consistently show up with high energy, patience, and the emotional resilience needed to support my team.

Self-Respect

Setting clear boundaries and honoring my word fosters mutual respect with colleagues, encouraging open and honest communication across the board.

Wisdom

Drawing on past experiences and listening deeply enables me to make thoughtful decisions, navigate complex dynamics, and approach conflict with perspective.

Purpose

Choosing work and commitments that align with my values helps me contribute meaningfully and leave a lasting impact.
